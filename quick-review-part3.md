# Comprehensive Interview Questions and Answers for .NET Technology Lead & Production Support Lead Roles

## .NET Technology Lead Role (ESP Modernization Project)

### General Questions
1. **What is your experience with React 18+?**  
   Discuss your familiarity with new features, such as concurrent rendering, and how they can optimize application performance.

2. **Can you describe your experience with .NET Core 9/10?**  
   Focus on enhancements, performance improvements, and your experience building microservices.

### Scenario-Based Questions
1. **How would you approach migrating a monolithic application to a microservices architecture?**  
   Discuss key considerations such as domain-driven design, service discovery, and data management.

2. **Describe a challenging situation you faced during a cloud migration to Azure or AWS.**  
   Explain the factors you considered and how you addressed them.

### Technical Deep Dives
1. **Explain how Docker can facilitate development and deployment in microservices architecture.**  
   Discuss container orchestration and how it relates to services scaling.

2. **What are the best practices for implementing CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins in a cloud environment?**  
   Explore pipelines, environment configurations, and testing strategies.

### Architecture Discussions
1. **What strategies would you adopt to optimize performance and scalability in a .NET Core microservices application deployed on Azure?**  
   Touch upon caching, load balancing, and asynchronous processing.

2. **How do you ensure security in microservices deployed on Kubernetes?**  
   Discuss the use of service meshes, JWT tokens, and API gateways.

### Migration Strategies
1. **If tasked with modernizing a legacy VB6 application, what approach would you take?**  
   Discuss the importance of understanding existing functionalities and considering a phased migration approach.

2. **How would you handle data migration from DB2 to a modern database system?**  
   Explain the tools and strategies for ensuring data integrity and minimal downtime.

### Leadership Questions
1. **How do you foster a culture of innovation in your team?**  
   Discuss your techniques for encouraging team collaboration and creativity.

2. **Can you provide an example of how you managed conflict within your team during a critical project?**  
   Highlight your mediation strategies and focus on maintaining team morale.

## Production Support Lead Role (Legacy Systems)

### General Questions
1. **What has been your experience with supporting legacy systems such as VB6 or DB2?**  
   Detail your strategies for maintaining and upgrading them alongside new technology.

2. **How do you prioritize incident management when dealing with multiple high-priority tickets?**  
   Discuss your approach and any frameworks you find useful.

### Scenario-Based Questions
1. **Describe a time you had to troubleshoot a critical incident involving a legacy system.**  
   Share how you approached the problem and involved stakeholders for resolution.

2. **What would you do if you identified a major flaw in a legacy system?**  
   Discuss data loss implications and how you would communicate this to leadership.

### Best Practices
1. **How do you handle continuous improvement in support processes?**  
   Explain metrics you track and how they inform your team's practices.

2. **What role does documentation play in supporting legacy systems?**  
   Talk about knowledge transfer and training new staff members.

---

This document aims to prepare candidates for comprehensive interviews focusing on .NET Technology Lead roles with an emphasis on newer technologies and modernization efforts, as well as Production Support Lead roles that involve legacy systems and incident management.
