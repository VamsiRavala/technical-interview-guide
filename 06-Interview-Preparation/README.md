## 📊 Assessment

Before starting, assess your current level:

### Beginner
- < 1 year of professional experience
- Limited exposure to the technology
- Need to build fundamentals

### Intermediate
- 1-3 years of professional experience
- Comfortable with core concepts
- Ready to deepen knowledge

### Advanced
- 3+ years of professional experience
- Strong foundation
- Focus on advanced patterns and optimization
